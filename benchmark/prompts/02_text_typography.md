# Text and Typography

---

## P-01 — Fashion Poster
**Difficulty:** Medium

Create a premium minimalist fashion poster.

Use a subtle fashion-inspired background only. The background should support the design without competing with the text.

### Include exactly:

**NEW SEASON**

**AUTUMN / WINTER 2026**

Modern Form. Timeless Style.

**DISCOVER NOW**

Use elegant editorial typography with strong hierarchy and generous spacing.

Text and typography must be the main focus.

Do not let background elements overlap or obstruct the text.

Do not add any other text.
