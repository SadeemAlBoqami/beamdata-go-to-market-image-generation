# Branding and Advertising

## BA-01
**Difficulty:** Easy
> Create a product advertisement for a fictional skincare brand called "LUMI". Show a single glass serum bottle centered on a baby blue background, with soft shadows and a clean minimal look.

## BA-02
**Difficulty:** Medium
> Create a social media advertisement for a fictional sportswear brand called "VORA". Show a white sneaker on a dark pedestal with orange accents, dramatic side lighting, and negative space on the left for advertising copy.

## BA-03
**Difficulty:** Medium
> Create a promotional advertisement for a fictional coffee brand called "NOVA". Show a takeaway coffee cup beside scattered coffee beans on a warm beige surface. Use soft morning lighting, a modern premium style, and include the brand name "NOVA" clearly on the cup.

## BA-04
**Difficulty:** Hard
> Create a luxury advertising campaign for a fictional skincare brand called "AURA". Place one transparent serum bottle in the center on a white stone pedestal, with two smaller cosmetic jars positioned behind it, one on each side. Add white flowers and subtle water reflections around the products. Use soft golden lighting and a beige-and-white color palette. Include the headline "Reveal Your Glow" at the top and the brand name "AURA" at the bottom. Do not include any additional text.

## BA-05
**Difficulty:** Hard
> Create a bold social media campaign for a fictional energy drink brand called "PULSE". Show three beverage cans arranged at different heights on metallic platforms: a black can in the center, an orange can on the left, and a silver can on the right. Add splashing water and orange light streaks in the background. Include the headline "POWER YOUR MOMENT" at the top and the brand name "PULSE" clearly on all three cans. Leave clean negative space in the lower-right corner for additional campaign content.
